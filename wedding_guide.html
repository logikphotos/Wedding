Getting there

Fly into Valencia, then rent a car. It's by far the easiest way to reach Vilafamés and get around once you're there.

Rental car is the way to go

Valencia to Vilafamés is about an hour by car. Vilafamés has no train station and is not reachable by rail — a car (or a pre-arranged taxi) is genuinely the only practical way to get there and back, and it also makes exploring the village and surrounding area much easier once you've arrived.

1
Land in Valencia (VLC)

Pick up a rental car at the airport, or take the metro into the city first (about 25 minutes) and rent one later.

2
Optional time in Valencia

If you're staying in the city for a few days beforehand, no car is needed there — it's easily walkable, with metro and taxis for everything else.

3
Drive to Vilafamés — about 1 hour

The direct drive from Valencia to Vilafamés is roughly an hour. Trains do run from Valencia to Castellón de la Plana, but Castellón is as far as the rail line goes — Vilafamés itself has no train service, so a car for this leg is essentially required.

In Valencia

A big, walkable, sun-drenched city — plan for at least a full day here.

City of Arts & Sciences

Santiago Calatrava's futuristic complex of museums, an aquarium, and an IMAX dome — Valencia's most photographed corner.

Half a day  ·  easy by metro
Mercado Central

One of Europe's oldest working markets, under a beautiful Art Nouveau iron-and-glass roof. Go hungry — jamón, fresh juice, and local produce everywhere.

Morning visit recommended
Turia Gardens

A former riverbed turned nine-kilometer park that winds through the whole city — great for a run, a stroll, or cutting between neighborhoods.

Free  ·  always open
Barrio del Carmen

The old town's tangle of narrow streets, street art, tapas bars, and small plazas — best explored slowly, ideally after dark.

Evening  /  tapas crawl
Paella, properly

Valencia is its birthplace — the traditional version uses rabbit and chicken, not seafood. Worth seeking out a spot that makes it to order; it takes time, and that's the sign it's real.

Lunch, not dinner — it's a midday dish here

In Vilafamés

A hilltop village of about 1,500 people, built from the same warm pink-toned stone as its castle. Small enough to see in an afternoon, atmospheric enough to want longer.

The old town & castle

Wander up through the medieval streets to the ruined 11th-century Castell de Vilafamés at the summit — the walk itself, past red-stone houses and stepped alleys, is the main event.

Wear shoes you can climb cobblestones in
MACVAC — Museum of Contemporary Art

A genuinely excellent modern art museum for a village this size, housed in a former palace at the base of the old town.

Tue–Sun  ·  roughly 10–2 & 4–6:30
La Roca Grossa

A striking mass of reddish rock right in the heart of the village — one of Vilafamés's most recognizable landmarks and a good orientation point.

Santa María church

A 16th-century church partway up the climb to the castle, with a reddish façade and a small viewpoint beside it looking out over the valley.

Local wine

The area sits on a small wine route through the surrounding hills, ending at a bodega in the village itself — worth a stop if anyone's around a day early.

Day-of essentials

A few practical things about the venue and the village that will save you a headache on Saturday.

⚡
Bring a plug adapter

Spain uses the two-round-pin Europlug (Type C/F), 230V. Pack a travel adapter — and a portable charger, since phones will be working hard for photos all day.

📶
No WiFi at the venue

The summer home where the ceremony is held doesn't have WiFi. Download offline maps, playlists, and anything you'll want on hand beforehand, and let anyone expecting to reach you know you may be offline for the day.

🅿️
Park in the designated area

Parking is in the village's designated lot — Aparcamiento, Vilafamés — not at the house itself (Carrer Mesón, 9). It's free, but plan a short walk from there and build in a few extra minutes.

👟
Comfortable shoes for cobblestones

Both the village and the walk from parking involve uneven stone streets — worth factoring into your outfit, especially for heels.

☀️
October weather

Expect warm, sunny days (upper 60s–70s°F) and cooler evenings — a light layer for after sunset is a good idea.
